# KYNTRAZE: FRACTURE TACTICS — shared design and engineering contract

Use this document with every B38–B43 prompt. Copy the approved version into `docs/project/TACTICS-DESIGN.md` in B38. Update it only for documented, justified decisions, with matching tests and data changes. Each next batch reads it and the previous status before acting. These are implementation specifications, not claims about existing features.

## Product and scope

A complete, separate, single-player turn-based anime JRPG inside KYNTRAZE. Fracture Rush remains the existing real-time action roguelite; neither mode replaces the other. Use Attack of the Saiyans for expressive 2D party battles and timed defense, Expedition 33 for cinematic reactions, and Octopath Traveler II for rich layered staging and enemy tells. Borrow principles, never proprietary art, music, characters, exact interfaces or formulas. Four canonical heroes; three active unique party members and one optional reserve outside combat. Side-view with slight depth, heroes left and enemies right, not overhead. First finish a battle vertical slice, then expand to a complete compact campaign with playable exploration. No networked tactics, PvP, gacha, new heroes, additional transformations or unrelated Rush changes. The rejected Enemy Uprising batch is NOT a dependency.

## Battle rules v1 (initial tuning, playtest and adjust centrally)

### Party, statistics and clock

1–3 active heroes, 1–4 enemies initially, fixed readable slots. No grid/pathfinding. Grounded/airborne is a tactical state only where a technique explicitly uses it. HP, Energy (0–100), ATK, POW, DEF, SPD, crit (fraction), Drive (0–100), Overdrive (0–100). No hidden dodge RNG by default. Each actor has nextAct tick, stable tie-break by actor ID after higher SPD. At start nextAct = ceil(1000/SPD). Pick smallest nextAct; advance logical time to it, not elapsed seconds. After action nextAct = currentTick + max(1, ceil(1000 * delay / effectiveSPD)); no actions while actor KO. Freeze ordering during input/reaction/cinema. Display next 6–8 projected actors (intent and speed changes can update projection; no false certainty). Round label, if used, is a UI grouping of logical ticks, not a separate effect clock. Baseline stats at level 1:

| Hero | HP | ATK | POW | DEF | SPD | Crit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Kai | 500 | 52 | 48 | 32 | 100 | .08 |
| Ren | 560 | 58 | 40 | 38 | 92 | .07 |
| Sora | 440 | 38 | 62 | 25 | 112 | .10 |
| Vex | 490 | 50 | 50 | 34 | 104 | .09 |

Ordinary encounter enemies start around 150–340 HP and 35–55 attack equivalent; tune solo vs party encounter compositions independently, not by copying Rush counts. Enemy tiers and bosses use authored data. Baseline start at 60 Energy, 0 Drive, 0 OD (B38 teaching scenario may supply explicitly labeled starting Drive; normal fights must also allow organically earning it). Initiative and enemy intents visible before commitment.

### Commands and targeting

Attack (physical or hero-specific ranged basic, delay 1.0, total multiplier ~1.0, gain 15 Energy and 15 Drive); Techniques (Q/E/R plus signature as named cards, no dependency on keyboard Q/E/R); Guard (delay .85, next hostile action damage ×.55, gain 10 Energy and 10 Drive); Charge (delay 1.0, +35 Energy, +25 Drive); Transform (delay .9); Overdrive (delay .9, transformed only); items and linked attacks unlock in B42. Technique starter bands: light cost 12–18, total 1.2–1.6, delay .9–1.05; medium cost 22–30, total 1.7–2.3, delay 1.1–1.3; heavy cost 35–45, total 2.5–3.5 single target or 1.5–2.3 per target AoE, delay 1.35–1.6. Use explicit per-target and per-hit coefficients (sum equals intended total), declared target modes self/ally/single enemy/all enemies/adjacent slots, visible preview and legal targets. No automatic extra damage for decorative particles. Signature and ultimate distinct if source has both. Cooldowns measured in own future decisions skipped (set N, unavailable for next N decisions, decrement once at end of those decisions, not on cast); typical heavy N=1–2, not converted seconds. On a damaging technique gain 10 Drive once per action, not per hit. On taking positive damage gain 8 Drive once per hostile action, not per hit. Cap all resources, reject invalid/duplicate commands before spending. Self buff/counter must have genuine usefulness; positional moves adapt to discrete states, not imaginary terrain coordinates. A defeated target stops receiving damage, remaining strikes follow explicit retarget policy (default no retarget); damage/loot attribution cannot double count.

### Damage, status and timing

For each actual hit use `max(1, round((ATK or POW) * coefficient * (100/(100+max(0,DEF))) * formDamage * weakness * crit * timing * guard * other))` for valid damaging hits; immunity, perfect dodge or canceled hit = 0, shield absorbs after calculation. No min-1 through immunity. Crit seeded once per damaging action by default (×1.5), preview displays noncrit damage and crit possibility, not exact hidden RNG. Weak/resist 1.25/.75 unless explicit boss rules. No random variance initially. Guard ×.55 for next hostile action, does not stack with itself; expires at end of that hostile action or next own turn start, whichever first. Perfect attack ×1.15, ordinary/missed prompt ×1.0; never punish normal command selection. Reaction on enemy telegraphed hit: Dodge perfect = 0; Parry perfect = ×.25 and bounded counter (if legal, ×.6 basic coefficient, one per hostile action, no recursive counter); good defense ×.7; miss/timeout ×1.0. Guard composes multiplicatively where appropriate. Every dangerous enemy move declares dodge/parry eligibility and target(s) up front. One defensive prompt per hostile action by default, applies to indicated eligible targeted heroes; multi-hit visuals don't force repeated button spam. Start with 600ms input window, perfect central 160ms, good central 400ms; readable anticipatory cue before window, accessible extended timing and automatic Standard defense option (×.7 on eligible attacks) with no progression penalty. Timing mode can be changed outside a pending prompt. Input is sampled by presenter monotonic clock, submitted as validated discrete grade bound to action ID, replayable; no engine dependency on wall time, no duplicate key repeat or early hold. Pause/blur freezes cue/time; reduced effects/skip cannot remove essential cue. In a genuinely noninteractive mode use deterministic default. Never ask the player to time a cutscene at variable frame rate.

Status definitions specify stack limit, owner, duration clock and exact triggers. Standard duration: N affected actor future turns, tick/expire at end of those turns, not application turn; delayed detonation and break exceptions explicitly data-driven. DOT cannot recurse or trigger indefinite gain. Stun skips at most one turn, bosses resistant and immune to repeated turn locks; each actor can only counter once per originating action. Ash Brands (max 3 per target, consumed for capped burst); Star Traces (max 3 per target for initial RPG balance, bounded aggregate consumption); Vex marks and parry payoff; Kai precision and evasion; make all visible, do not just add damage multipliers. Break/exposure: authored enemy guard gauge, weak/interrupt techniques reduce it; on zero expose target ×1.25 until end of its next own turn, reset gauge, boss has one-turn break resistance. Do not allow endless break chains. Status and intent tooltips explain exact clocks and immunity.

### Transformations and companions

13 kits: Kai base/Flarebound/Cometheart/Stillfire Apex; Ren base/Crimson Ascendant/Ashen Eclipse; Sora base/Azure Sovereign/Eventide Seraph; Vex base/Riftbound/Voidheart Exile. Transformation changes visuals, kit, passive and stats; no new forms. Unlock through Tactics progression only (early batches use explicit battle scenario unlocks, no Rush writes). Form I entry threshold 65 for Kai, 100 for others; higher tiers 100. Use Drive as maintained gauge, not threshold-sized immediate payment: entry cost 20, own action end drain 10/15/20 by tier including subsequent actions only (not entry action); damage ×1.30/1.60/1.90 initial ceiling, SPD ×1.05/1.08/1.10, no max HP refill or infinite resource loops. Active forms earn half normal Drive (rounded down), no Drive from Charge while transformed (Energy still gains), enforce at most 6/5/4 subsequent owner turns per tier; when drain depletes or limit expires revert after current action. Manual revert consumes turn, switching form same entry requirements, no free reset or chain refreshing while transformed (revert first); cooldowns persist by ability ID. Transform cinematics commit state once at declared beat; reduced effects and skip yield same state. KO clears form and OD, never restores HP by form switching.

B41 familiars: one small living companion per hero, non-targetable by default, no initiative slot, no collision; one assist after eligible damaging owner action, cooldown one intervening owner turn, ~.30–.45 basic equivalent, distinct effects, no meter generation, no assists from assists/counters/links. Owner commands are primary. OD fills only while transformed (+20 on damaging owner action, +10 on being hit once/action), threshold 100, consume full meter, two subsequent owner actions, +25% damage (multiplicative), form drain/expiry continues; merge familiar into form-specific beast mantle at activation, no separate assist or sprite while merged, reform only when alive and not merged. No duplicate pet on KO/revive, victory or retry. OD does not auto-repeat; no meter gain while active. Separate from normal ultimate. B38–40 hide unavailable future feature commands rather than dead buttons.

### Outcomes and progression

Victory when all hostile actors (including required boss parts) are defeated, defeat when no active hero remains; settle action atomically, explicit draw policy = defeat if both sides wiped. KO is stable slot, revive only by defined item or story rule. Rewards exactly once per encounter ID/transaction. No state mutation by VFX. Use versioned JSON save with validation/migration, corruption backup, safe failure on unsupported version, read-after-write checks, two generations or equivalent recoverable save. Save at safe decision/exploration boundaries, no pending reaction/cinematic; suspend at next safe boundary; never reroll seed on resume. Persist active battle snapshot/seed/RNG/clock/cooldowns/status/intents/reward receipt, no callbacks/DOM. Clear completed snapshot atomically with reward claim. Isolate QA and production stores. B38 supports simple safe practice session; B42 adds full progression; B43 integrates world.

## Presentation contract

Premium 2D anime JRPG, not dashboard. 16:9 target composition with layered illustrated backgrounds, legible foreground, parallax, contact shadows, coherent side lighting, authored staging and side-facing sprites (test existing east/west sheets at ~150–210 visible px on 1080p; preserve alpha, nearest sampling where appropriate, no blurred enlargement). Assess source resolution and posture; generate/refine new consistent poses only when needed; no independent random frames that change anatomy, clothes or face. Rig/cutout/export from reproducible layered source for sequences; all frames aligned to feet/hands/weapon anchors. ImageGen generously for backgrounds, sprites/parts, FX textures, technique icons, portraits, key art, decorative UI; integrate actual assets, not screenshots as interface or fake generation. Keep generated art clean, original, production-ready, correct alpha; document provenance and permissions. Prefer reusable atlas/manifest and scoped loading. If image/audio tool unavailable, report and leave visual gate open, never substitute ugly placeholders as accepted output.

HUD: upper turn queue/intent, enemy HP and break/status/target indicators near sprites without overlapping art, lower compact party cards HP/Energy/Drive/OD, active hero highlight, command rail, illustrated technique subpanel with costs/cooldown/targets/preview. Keyboard arrows/confirm/back, pointer and gamepad, visible focus and controller hints; cancel until commit, input locked while resolving except active reaction/skip. Camera focuses on attacker, target and impact, returns to exact staging. One consistent event choreography (anticipation → motion/travel → contact/resolve display → reaction → recovery), per-hit floating numbers matching simulation, KO/victory after final beats, no duplicate audio. Basics brisk (~0.5–1.2s), techniques ~1–2.5s, signature ~2–4s, transformations/ultimate ~3–6s first show; repeat/fast mode shortens presentation only, not rules or cues. Use sound layering, impact pause, controlled shake, lighting and dissipating effects. Reduce flashes/shake and preserve meaningful signals in reduced-effects mode. No full-screen noise over enemy cues. Handle resize, focus, teardown, input buffering, pause, reduced motion and repeated scene mount. 1920×1080, 1280×720, 960×540 and small 640×360 readable; safe scroll or alternate panels where necessary, no clipped controls. Browser and Electron share behavior.

Record short normal-speed clips if environment permits, don't claim unseen/heard verification.
