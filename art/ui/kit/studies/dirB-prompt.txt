Text-free game UI material study for a dark sci-fi 2D action roguelite called a fractured-relic world (illustrated, pixel-adjacent crisp edges, painterly shading, light from top-left). Flat orthographic front view, NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logos, no screenshots of a game. Neutral plain dark charcoal #101418 background. Show on one sheet: a wide thin health-bar shell (empty, with a channel inside), a row of four square ability-slot frames, a rectangular menu panel frame with corner plates, one large primary button face and one secondary button face (both empty), a small square minimap frame with corner brackets. Palette: deep ink and muted steel base, disciplined teal/cyan energy accents, controlled amber for primary actions, no rainbow, no big blooms. Direction B 'Shard Glass': angular crystalline shard frames, slate and etched stone, hairline cyan light edges, asymmetrical diagonal slashes, faceted glassy bevels.
