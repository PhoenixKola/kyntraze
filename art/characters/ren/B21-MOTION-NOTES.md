- **Hurt (8f):** no hop; feet stay planted and are dragged along the push (7-13 px), torso whips first (spine -16 -> -20 deg, head -26 deg peak), then hunches. Profile and back views equivalent (profile: spine 24 deg, head 26 deg). Death's first two keys share these poses.
- **Run (8f):** centre of mass ~1 px lower (HIP_DY), stronger shoulder-led counter-swing (SOUTH spine 3 -> 4.5 deg, profile 2 -> 3.2) with head counter-rotation. Leg tables and foot-contact frames unchanged. RunLower and RunPunchUpper are generated from the same Run cycle, so leg phase still matches (RunLower/RunPunchUpper crop origins moved by 1 px with the lower hips; frame counts unchanged).
- **Dash (10f, session 3):** deeper launch crouch (pelvis +5 px, chest forward lean), stronger forward lean through the stretched mid frames (SOUTH spine -8 -> -12; profile 34 -> 40), heavier landing absorb (pelvis 11/14/10 -> 15/19/13, small forward chest dip). Event frames (anticipation 0, burst 2, travel_end 6, recover 7), fps 22, frame count unchanged. Crop size/origin shifted 1-2 px (SOUTH 134x168 -> 132x167).
- **Death (18f, session 3):** an extra knee-buckle beat at frame 3 between the stagger and the kneel (hip drops ~22 px, spine whips), deeper kneel (+6 px) before the tip-back; ground contact 10, settled 14 and the final hold unchanged. Starts from the shared Hurt keys.
