Pixel-art effects sheet for a 2D three-quarter-overhead action game: reusable, separate transparent energy effects for a giant energy-sphere EXPLOSION and its CHARGING / LANDING-ZONE telegraph. They will be tinted any colour in the engine, so draw them in NEUTRAL tones only. The attached image shows the game's pixel-art style and block size from an earlier effects sheet: FINE clean pixel blocks (each art pixel a clear 5x5 block), no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no sub-pixel noise, hard stepped pixel shading, crisp readable contours. Seen from the same slightly-from-above game camera. Original designs, not copied from any existing franchise.

COLOUR RULE: use ONLY these neutral tones: pure white (#ffffff) for the hottest core, very light grey-blue (#dfe6f2), light-mid grey-blue (#bac5dc), mid grey-blue (#8c9ab8) and a dark grey-blue (#3a4560) for the outer edge. No other colours, no black outlines.

4. EMBER SPARKS: a loose cluster of 12 tiny separate flying sparks and ember chips of different sizes and brightness (2 to 7 art pixels each), spread over an area about 96x96.

GROUND SCORCH DECAL: a burnt blast mark on the floor seen from slightly above: an irregular dark elliptical scorch about twice as wide as tall, drawn in the DARKEST tones only (#3a4560 and an even darker #1f2638) with a few faint mid-tone crack lines and a ragged edge. About 160 wide and 80 tall.
