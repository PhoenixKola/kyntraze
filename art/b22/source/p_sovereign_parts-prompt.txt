Original 2D game art for the action-survival game KYNTRAZE: FRACTURE RUSH, 'Shattered Horizons' expansion. Chunky pixel-art style: clean dark 3-4 px outline, cel-shaded two-tone shading, crisp hard edges, no soft gradients, no blur, readable at small size. Seen from a slightly raised three-quarter top-down angle, creature facing right (east). Paradox palette: folded-dimension void, deep indigo / violet with teal-cyan glass and crystal highlights. Three separate parts of the Sovereign of the Void in one row on green, equal size: (1) a triangular ANCHOR crystal obelisk with a glowing core, (2) a large armoured ARM/gauntlet slab with lilac trim, (3) an inverted-triangle crown HEAD mask in pale lilac. Flat pure green (#00ff00) background, wide green gutters, no text, no green in subjects.
