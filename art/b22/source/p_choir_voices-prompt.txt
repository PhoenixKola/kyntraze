Original 2D game art for the action-survival game KYNTRAZE: FRACTURE RUSH, 'Shattered Horizons' expansion. Chunky pixel-art style: clean dark 3-4 px outline, cel-shaded two-tone shading, crisp hard edges, no soft gradients, no blur, readable at small size. Seen from a slightly raised three-quarter top-down angle, creature facing right (east). Paradox palette: folded-dimension void, deep indigo / violet with teal-cyan glass and crystal highlights. Three separate small spectral constructs (the Choir voices) in one row on green, equal size: (1) a tall thin pale lilac PILLAR-lance construct with a glowing tip, (2) a wide fan-shaped CONE construct like an open pale-violet bell/horn, (3) a RING construct made of two concentric pale-lilac rings. Same palette, all hollow and ghostly. Flat pure green (#00ff00) background, wide green gutters, no text, no green in subjects.
