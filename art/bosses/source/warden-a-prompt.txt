BOSS of the hostile "Fracture" family: dark obsidian / gunmetal crystalline shell plates with hot crimson-magenta (#ff3a6e) glowing energy seams and a large glowing core, bone-white highlight edges. NO orange or gold anywhere (gold belongs to the hero). The first reference image shows the hero rendering style (clean dark outline, cel-shaded two-tone shading, crisp hard edges): match that clean cel-shaded style with a consistent 3-4 px dark outline, flat shading steps, no soft gradients, no blur, no painterly noise. The second reference shows a smaller family enemy (the Breaker Sentinel): the boss must be clearly different and far larger. Seen from a slightly raised three-quarter top-down angle, body FACING RIGHT (east). Feet/contact at the bottom of each cell on one common ground line, SAME SCALE in every cell, same identity in every cell. Each cell: nothing cropped, character fully inside with margin.

Layout: EXACTLY 8 cells on flat magenta (#ff00ff) in 2 rows of 4, evenly spaced with wide magenta gutters, one pose per cell, no text, no labels, no grid lines, no shadows on the ground, no watermark. Wide 16:9 canvas.
