# Gloam Circuit arena art: provenance
All art is original ImageGen output (bridge `scripts/gen-image.py`, model gpt-6.1-sol, audited bridge hash), references: `ref-floor-tiles.png` (the shipped floor tiles) and `ref-kai-east.png` (scale). Prompts are `*-prompt.txt` (generated by `_make_prompts.py`). 7 sheets, 7 generations, all accepted on the first draft (props, decor, glyph, rim, fx, amb, card); none rejected.
Post-processing is scripted and reproducible: `python scripts/build-gloam-arena.py` (flood-fill key, connected-component extraction because the freight pod crosses the nominal cell grid, scale to the footprint, defringe, desaturate scorch/stripes/crack-b, panel-crop + vertical trim for the tileable rim, cross-fade tiling for the surge strips, luminance-to-alpha + seamless blend for the fog). Review: `python scripts/b09-gloam-review.py` -> `art/arenas/gloam/review/`.
Static art: nothing here is animated or rigged (the surge strips come in two variants for a 2-frame flicker).
